MNIST CNN Model Evaluation Metrics
========================================

Accuracy: 0.9877
Precision: 0.9879
Recall: 0.9874
F1-Score: 0.9876

Metric Definitions:
- Accuracy: The ratio of correct predictions to total predictions
- Precision: The ratio of true positive predictions to all positive predictions
- Recall: The ratio of true positive predictions to all actual positive cases
- F1-Score: The harmonic mean of precision and recall

Interpretation:
- High accuracy indicates the model correctly classifies most digits
- High precision means when the model predicts a digit, it's usually correct
- High recall means the model finds most instances of each digit
- F1-score balances precision and recall for overall performance
